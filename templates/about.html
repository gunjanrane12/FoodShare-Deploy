Our Story

We Believe No Good
Food Should Go to Waste

FoodShare was built from a simple truth: restaurants throw away millions of meals while millions of people go hungry. We're here to bridge that gap.

Why We Exist

Our Mission

FoodShare was created to reduce food waste while helping those in need. Our platform connects restaurants with surplus food to NGOs and charities that can distribute it to underserved communities.

By bridging this gap, we ensure that excess food is used efficiently to fight hunger, rather than going to waste. We believe every meal matters, and no food should be discarded when it can provide hope to someone else.
